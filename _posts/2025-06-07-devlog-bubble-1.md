# Devlog #1 - Introducing My Game: *Bubble Factory*

Hello everyone, I'm starting a devlog for my first full independent game project to practice my skills in game design and developement.

---

## Game Overview 
 - **Genre:** Cozy factory sim
 - **Engine** Unity
 - **Platform:** PC / Web
 - **Style:** Low Poly 3D
 - **Inspiration:** Oiligarchy, McDonald's Video Game

---

## Core Concept
The game is set in a world with a sudden demand for enjoyment of soapy bubbles for pleasure or entertainment. Not long after, an enterprising businessman sets out to build an entire production network to feed that new product niche.

---

## First Minute
- Quick logo splash
- Title Screen with soothing music, showing off the bubbly colors, small hint of a more “fun but sinister” outcome.
- Options: New Game / Continue / Settings
- New Game leads to a quick newspaper cover of the bubble boom and our player’s role in launching the factory.
- Player gains control on a small grid with one building available to start his factory.

---

## Features I'm exploring
- Grid based isometric puzzles
- Managing game economy
- Creating builder type game
- Narration through gameplay

---

## Art & Mood
I'm going for a low poly, positive vibe. The sinister elements of the game are meant to be concealed in the levity of the artstyle and are left for the player to pick up on as they play through the game.
